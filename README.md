# Multimodal AI for Personalized Medicine: Early Detection & Risk Triage of MASLD

A deep learning decision support system designed for early detection and severity grading of **Metabolic Dysfunction-Associated Steatotic Liver Disease (MASLD)**. 
By combining B-mode liver ultrasound images with clinical and laboratory data, this tool helps clinicians streamline case finding, improve early-stage diagnostic accuracy, and accelerate specialized referrals.

---

## 📌 Overview

MASLD affects approximately **38% of the global adult population** [1]. 
Early-stage MASLD frequently goes undetected because standard B-mode ultrasound exhibits low diagnostic sensitivity across the subtle boundary between a healthy liver (S0) and mild steatosis (S1). 
While higher-accuracy diagnostic procedures exist, they are often cost-prohibitive for routine early screening.

This project addresses this clinical gap by providing a cost-effective, high-accuracy decision support tool.
**It is designed not to replace clinicians, but to empower them** with actionable insights to optimize patient referral pathways and speed up specialized fibrosis assessments.

---

## 🔑 Key Features

* **Multimodal Integration:** Fuses visual features from B-mode ultrasound images with tabular clinical and laboratory data.
* **Deep Learning Classification:** Utilizes a Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) classifier for accurate stage grading.
* **Confidence Scoring:** Generates explicit confidence metrics alongside predictions to support clinical decision-making and risk triage.
* **Cost-Effective Early Triage:** Offers higher diagnostic accuracy than standard ultrasound alone without requiring high-cost secondary imaging.
